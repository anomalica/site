Overview

The pipeline

Anomalica turns raw source material into a public, fully-sourced knowledge base.
Read it top to bottom: raw sources are ingested into records, digested into per-record facts, assimilated into one knowledge graph, then assembled into articles and rendered to the site. The copyright boundary sits between the ingests (which hold the source text, so their copyright follows the source) and the digests onward (facts and claims, which can't be copyrighted and are public).
Click any box to see what it does, what it produces, and its copyright status.
