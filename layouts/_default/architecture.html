Overview

The pipeline

Anomalica turns raw source material into a public, fully-sourced knowledge base.
Read it top to bottom: raw sources are ingested into records, digested into per-record facts, assimilated into one knowledge graph, then assembled into articles and rendered to the site. The privacy boundary sits between the ingests (copyrighted source text, private) and the digests onward (structured facts, public).
