Luis Elizondo is a former United States intelligence officer who directed the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Programme (AATIP) from 2010 until his resignation in October 2017.<sup>1</sup>

Elizondo resigned from the [United States Department of Defence](/organisations/us-department-of-defence) citing frustration with what he described as excessive secrecy and internal opposition to investigating unidentified aerial phenomena.<sup>2</sup>

Following his resignation, Elizondo joined To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science, where he worked alongside former government officials to advocate for greater transparency around UAP encounters. His departure from government and subsequent public advocacy contributed to the broader disclosure movement that led to the establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office in 2022.
