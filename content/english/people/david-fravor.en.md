David Fravor is a retired United States Navy Commander who served as commanding officer of Strike Fighter Squadron 41 (VFA-41), also known as the Black Aces. He accumulated over 3,500 flight hours and had 18 years of active service at the time of the [2004 USS Nimitz encounter](/events/2004-uss-nimitz-encounter).

On 14 November 2004, Fravor was directed to investigate an unknown contact detected by the [USS Princeton](/objects/uss-princeton)'s SPY-1B radar system. Along with Lieutenant Commander Alex Dietrich, he observed a white, featureless object approximately 12 metres in length, shaped like a Tic Tac breath mint, hovering above a disturbance in the ocean surface.<sup>1</sup>

Fravor attempted to intercept the object by descending towards it. The object appeared to respond to his manoeuvres, mirroring his circular descent before accelerating away at a speed he described as beyond any technology known to him.<sup>2</sup> The object subsequently appeared at the combat air patrol point, approximately 95 kilometres away, within seconds of departing the encounter area.<sup>3</sup>

Fravor testified before the United States House of Representatives on 26 July 2023 as part of a hearing on unidentified anomalous phenomena.
