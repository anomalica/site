## Data collection

Anomalica does not collect personal data. There are no analytics trackers, no advertising scripts, no cookies, and no user accounts.

## Hosting

The site is served as static HTML via a content delivery network. Access logs are maintained by the hosting provider for operational purposes. Anomalica does not access or process these logs.

## Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log requests according to its own privacy policy. No other third-party services are loaded.

## Knowledge graph

The downloadable knowledge graph contains information extracted from publicly available source documents. It does not contain personal data about site visitors.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@anomalica.is.
